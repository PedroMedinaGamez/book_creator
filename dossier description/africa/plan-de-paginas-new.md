**## Story concept**



\- **\*\*Working title:\*\*** *\*Bo’s Map of Wonder\**

\- **\*\*Premise:\*\*** Grandpa’s glowing map sends Bo across Africa, where fifteen animal friends help him follow clues to a treasure that holds the memories of their kindness.

\- **\*\*Synopsis (150–250 words):\*\*** One evening, Grandpa’s old map begins to glow beside Bo’s window. A bright star twinkles over Africa, and a golden path appears. Bo packs his little bag, hugs Grandpa, and sets off. From the great mosque in Casablanca to the pyramids of Giza, every stop brings a new wonder and a new animal friend. Each friend shares a clue, and each clue leaves a special shape on Bo’s map. He discovers ancient arches, churches carved from rock, Kenya’s wide Maasai Mara grasslands, and Tanzania’s snowy Mount Kilimanjaro. Then the trail leads him past a flat-topped mountain, ancient stone walls, a roaring waterfall, winding wetlands, and towering desert dunes. A gorilla points him toward a quiet lagoon town, and a manatee sends him along a road of giant trees. At Madagascar’s Avenue of the Baobabs, Bo finally finds the treasure beneath the biggest tree: a journal and fifteen smooth stones. Each stone brings back a place, a friend, and a kind moment. Bo discovers that the greatest treasure is not gold—it is the friendship and memories gathered along the way.

\- **\*\*Central emotional message:\*\*** Curiosity helps us discover new places; kindness turns new friends into lasting memories.

\- **\*\*Treasure payoff:\*\*** The journal and smooth stones recall each place and friend. This fulfills the map’s promise without making the ending about wealth.
